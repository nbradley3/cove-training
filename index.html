Mojito16 oz pilsner
Lime juice3/4 oz
Simple1/2 oz
Mint leaves6 to 8
White rum2 oz
Soda waterTop

Method: Build in the glass before ice, no shaker

1. Build in the glass before ice, no shaker

2. Add lime, simple, and 6 to 8 mint leaves, lightly press the mint, do not shred it

3. Add white rum, fill with ice, top with soda water

4. Quick stir, garnish with a full mint sprig and lime wedge

Garnish

Full mint sprig and lime wedge

Deerfield Classic Old FashionROX glass with a cube
Simple1/4 oz
Angostura bitters2 dashes
Orange bitters1 dash
Bourbon2 oz

Method: Stir with a spoon

1. Add simple, Angostura, and orange bitters

2. Add the cube to the glass

3. Add bourbon and stir with a spoon

4. Twist orange peel oil over the top and around the rim, drop in the peel

5. Finish with a bourbon cherry

Garnish

Orange peel oil around the rim, peel dropped in, bourbon cherry
